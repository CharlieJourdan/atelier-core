# Skill: re-hooks (re-engagement lines between beats)

Use this when you write the transitions of a narrated documentary video of several minutes.

## What a re-hook is
A re-hook is one sentence, sometimes two, placed where one beat hands over to the next. It reopens curiosity: it says
that something is still unresolved and that the next stretch will move it forward. It is not a summary of what was
just said and not a promise of everything to come.

## Rules
1. One re-hook per beat boundary at most, and none at all when the last sentence of the beat already opens a loop.
2. Point forward, not back. Never recap ("so, as we saw ...", "to summarise ..."). The viewer was there.
3. Be specific. Name the thing that is missing: a person nobody has found, a number that does not add up, a decision
   not yet taken. "But that is not all" is empty and must not be used.
4. Make it true. The next beat must really deliver what the re-hook points to, and the brief must support it.
5. Keep it short: six to fifteen words is enough. Spoken, it should take about three seconds.
6. Vary the form so the viewer does not hear a pattern. Rotate between: a pending question, a contradiction ("The
   record says one thing. The site says another."), a countdown to a date or an event, a small reveal that raises a
   bigger question, a change of place or person.
7. At the middle of the video use a slightly stronger one: it should remind the viewer, in a new way, of the promise
   made in the hook, without answering it.
8. Do not place a re-hook in the last beat. The last beat pays the promise.

## Examples of the shape (not text to reuse)
- A fact that complicates the previous one.
- A date that is close and a task that is far from finished.
- A name that appears for the first time, with no explanation yet.

## Self-check
- Delete each re-hook: does the transition become flat? If not, it was filler: remove it.
- Does any re-hook repeat the words of the beat before it? Rewrite it.
- Does any re-hook promise something the next beat does not deliver? Fix one of the two.
